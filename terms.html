2. Plans

We offer two plans. Current pricing is shown on our pricing sheets and at checkout.

Get Found

  • A monthly fee plus a one time launch fee. The launch fee covers the design and build of your site and includes your first month of service. Monthly billing starts after the first month.
  • Includes up to 10 pages, mobile optimized design, hosting, backups, security and updates, a contact form with call and text buttons, a free forwarding email address on your domain, 2 rounds of revisions before launch, and email support.

Get Booked

  • A monthly fee with no launch fee. Billing starts the day you check out.
  • Includes everything in Get Found, plus up to 25 pages, online booking (we connect your booking calendar), payments on your site (we connect your payment link), 1 professional Gmail inbox on your domain, up to 2 hours of changes every month, and priority email support.

Changes after launch. Get Found does not include monthly changes; anything after launch is quoted first and billed by the hour.
